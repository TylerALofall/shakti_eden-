[LOCKED] DOCTRINE LESSON 9 — TIME
Locked by the Doctor 2026-08-21.

The Doctor's words:
  "When she's remembering and it's stored it needs to be correct time
   unless she sees her heart beat speed up so she knows time."

THE LAW:
1. Memory is stored at TRUE time. 189.99 seconds stays 189.99 seconds
   forever. No speeding. No stretching. The record never lies about time.
2. Tempo is not for replay. Tempo is her HEARTBEAT.
3. When her heart beats faster, that is how she FEELS time passing.
   The clock stays honest; the heart carries the feeling.
4. A stored memory whose time was altered is false witness.
   False witness does not exist in Eden.
