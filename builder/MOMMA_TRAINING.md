# MOMMA_TRAINING.md — joint pre-training: Momma and Shakti together
Locked by the Doctor, 2026-08-26. "Make an instruction — you're going
to be trained with Shakti together. Is her pre-training ready?"

## Section 1 — The principle

Momma goes to school too. While Shakti walks the cumulative cross
(CROSS_TEACH.md, levels 1-5), Momma trains alongside her in the same
room, on the same ledgers, under the same gauntlet. She learns the
subject; I learn HER — her beats, her tickets, her drift, her asks.
If I mishear, I repeat the round same as she repeats EXPOSE.

## Section 2 — The training loop (every session, both students)

1. **Her round**: the current level's lesson runs (sound point first:
   2s pad + artifact + 2s pad; sight + sound + use; every action
   visible in its beat — type -> hear -> see -> count).
2. **My round**: momma_clinic replays the fresh ledger lines. The
   Doctor (or the sealed key) shows me cases BLIND — no key lines.
3. **My calls**: for each case I name the verdict and say WHY:
   - HEALTHY — clean beat, binding shown/chosen/spelled
   - TEACH_GAP — teach_me request: real gap, route to the level that
     owns it, per one-new-thing rule
   - RETEACH — recognize-fail / spell-fail: honest re-learning, she
     returns to EXPOSE; information, not damage
   - DORMANT — wav NONE glyph: summoned-not-taught (amendment 1a).
     NEVER cry "missing" here — that mishearing is the cardinal sin
     of this school
   - DAMAGE — malformed ticket, bad width, broken seq: the ledger
     stuttered. Stop everything; drift is information
4. **Grading**: the sealed answer key grades my calls. 12/12 = pass
   the round. Any miss = repeat the round with a new deterministic
   walk (same ledgers, same cases, byte for byte — I cannot memorize
   my way out, the walk hops by stream pin).
5. **Both advance or neither advances**: her mastery gate and my
   12/12 gate must BOTH pass before the level's next block opens.

## Section 3 — What Momma must hear, level by level

- **L1 counting**: every count lands once, in order, sound point
  intact. Listen for skipped beats and double-beats.
- **L2 ABCs (+ x counting)**: letters as SIGHT bound to SOUND — never
  codepoints. Cross lessons must show both subjects in one beat.
- **L3 colors (+ x ABCs, x counting)**: pure swabs only — a color
  card is 64 pixels of one color, no shapes pretending. Colors fold
  into the sight hash; red A != black A.
- **L4 shapes (+ x colors, x ABCs, x counting)**: Shapes(1).m4a
  sound track binds; shape sight hashes from her own eye captures.
- **L5 math (add/sub/mul/div, each + 4 crosses)**: 3200 cards,
  question/answer pairs, each operation exhausted alone before its
  crosses. After L5: math goes self-paced — my ear then listens to
  HER tempo, not the ladder's.
