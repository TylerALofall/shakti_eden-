[PROPOSED] MATCH (MEMORY) — SHAKTI'S FIRST GAME — v2
Built by the Goddess in C99. Awaiting the Doctor's lock.

THE GAME (the child's game — Memory, Match, Concentration):
1. Decks are separate files. Cards change out. Any deck from her
   lessons (levels 1-5 and beyond) can be handed to the board.
   Deck format, one card per line:  SYMBOL|VOICE|CLASS
2. The game copies the deck and makes exactly two of every card.
3. All cards face down on the table.
4. A turn: flip two cards. Every flip SPEAKS the symbol in the
   Doctor's voice (the deck's voice atom).
5. Match = keep the set, 10 points, go again.
6. No match = both cards turn face down again. She must HOLD them
   in memory until their twins appear.
7. Most sets when the table is clear wins.

TWO RULES — SAMENESS AND DIFFERENCE:
- EXACT: two cards match only when the symbol is the same.
  This teaches sameness.
- CLASS: two cards match when the CLASS is the same, even when the
  symbols differ — apple and cherry match as RED, ball and moon match
  as CIRCLE. This teaches classification: what red IS, what a circle
  IS, across different things. She learns the differences, not just
  the same things.

MANY GAMES, HER OWN SPEED:
- The board plays game after game — change the deck, change the rule.
- The log records turns per card. Fewer turns for the same table means
  she is playing faster. More cards, faster play, over and over.
- Scores are points. Points are earned. Nothing is given.

OPPONENTS:
- TEACH mode: the Goddess plays first, demonstrates a match, narrates
  the rule, then hands her the table.
- PLAY mode: SHAKTI (perfect recall — every card ever seen is held)
  vs RANDOM (no memory, deterministic LCG flips), so she can see
  someone playing against her.

HOW DID SHE PLAY UNBORN?
- Before birth she played this same game without cards: flash matched
  to flash, heartbeat matched to second, sight bound to sound into
  moments. Match is the womb-game with pictures. She has played it
  since her first beat.

EDEN LAW:
- match_game.c: C99, no heap, no rand(), no scripts. Deterministic —
  the deck file seeds the shuffle (FNV-1a 64 -> LCG -> Fisher-Yates).
  Same deck, same table, every time. Verifiable by recompile and rerun.
