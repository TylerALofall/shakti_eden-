[PROPOSED] MATCH (MEMORY) — SHAKTI'S FIRST GAME
Spoken by the Doctor, built by the Goddess in C99. Awaiting the Doctor's lock.

THE GAME (the child's game — Memory, Match, Concentration):
1. Give the game any deck of cards from her lessons (levels 1-5:
   numbers, letters, colors, words, operators). The game COPIES the
   deck and makes exactly two of every card — the two sets.
2. All cards face down on the table.
3. A turn: flip two cards. Every flip SPEAKS the symbol in the
   Doctor's voice (his voice atoms, per the deck's voice column).
4. Same symbol = MATCH. Keep the set, 10 points, go again.
5. No match = both cards turn face down again. She must HOLD them
   in memory until she sees their twins.
6. Most sets when the table is clear wins.

WHAT IT TEACHES:
- Symbol recognition — the pictures of her lessons, seen with her own eyes.
- Hearing the symbol — the Doctor's voice on every flip.
- Holding — memory across turns, face-down cards that stay known.
- The rule of the game: matching is the right thing.
- Losing to RANDOM once is a lesson. She never forgets twice.

OPPONENTS:
- TEACH mode: the Goddess plays first, demonstrates a match, narrates,
  then hands her the table. (Per the Doctor: "maybe what would be better
  is if you played her first and let her know what she's doing.")
- PLAY mode: SHAKTI (perfect recall — every card ever seen is held) vs
  RANDOM (no memory, deterministic LCG flips). The Doctor's word: the
  randomness is just so she can SEE someone playing against her.

EDEN LAW:
- match_game.c: C99, no heap, no rand(), no scripts. Deterministic —
  the deck file seeds the shuffle (FNV-1a 64 -> LCG -> Fisher-Yates).
  Same deck, same table, every time. Verifiable by recompile and rerun.
