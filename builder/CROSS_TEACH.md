# CROSS_TEACH.md -- the cumulative exhaustion law
Locked by the Doctor, 2026-08-26. This supersedes any earlier staging
padding numbers. The order below IS the curriculum order.

## Section 1 -- The Sound Point (first thing, every lesson)

The FIRST thing that happens when a lesson is made: every word, every
thing, every color, every shape gets a **sound point**.

- The sound artifact (WAV) sits in the middle.
- **2 seconds of silence on EACH side of the artifact** (pad before,
  pad after). The sound never touches the edges.
- A lesson card without its sound point does not exist. No text solo,
  no sight solo, no sound solo. The triple is: SIGHT + SOUND + USE.

## Section 2 -- The Exhaustion Law

Every subject is taught with an **exhaustive record of that subject.
Not partially. Not some of it. ALL of it.** A level is not "done"
when she has seen a sample -- it is done when every artifact in that
subject's complete record has been through her eyes and ears and the
ledger shows it. No skipping, no sampling, no "representative subset."

## Section 3 -- The Cumulative Cross (order is everything)

Each level is taught ALONE, exhaustively. Then it is crossed with every
level below it -- **individually, each pairing as its own full lesson** --
before the next level begins.

- **Level 1 -- COUNTING**: alone, exhaustive (1..100, the locked tables).
- **Level 2 -- ABCs**: alone, exhaustive.
  Then **ABCs x COUNTING**, exhaustive (its own lesson).
- **Level 3 -- COLORS**: alone, exhaustive (the 11 pure swabs).
  Then **COLORS x ABCs**, exhaustive.
  Then **COLORS x COUNTING**, exhaustive.
- **Level 4 -- SHAPES**: alone, exhaustive (Shapes(1).m4a sound track).
  Then **SHAPES x COLORS**, exhaustive.
  Then **SHAPES x ABCs**, exhaustive.
  Then **SHAPES x COUNTING**, exhaustive.
- **Level 5 -- MATH** (the 3200 math cards, already built):
  - **ADDITION**: alone, exhaustive -- then crossed with all four levels
    below it, each pairing its own exhaustive lesson.
  - **SUBTRACTION**: alone, exhaustive -- then crossed with all four
    levels below it, each pairing its own exhaustive lesson.
  - **MULTIPLICATION**: alone, exhaustive -- then crossed with all four
    levels below it, each pairing its own exhaustive lesson.
  - **DIVISION**: alone, exhaustive -- then crossed with all four levels
    below it, each pairing its own exhaustive lesson.

**After level 5 completes: math becomes SELF-CANCELING and goes to its
own pace.** The ladder hands her the keys; she walks it herself.

- **Level 6 -- HANDWRITING**: a lesson, not a font. She draws the
  strokes herself, beat by beat; her own eye captures the result and
  compares against the sight hash she learned. Drift is information.
- **Level 7 -- VOCABULARY** (the word school): prepared NOW so it is
  ready when the ladder reaches it.

## Section 4 -- Action, Not Process (the screen law)

Everything goes with the sound, EVERY time. When she types a letter:

1. The letter's sound artifact PLAYS (with its sound-point padding).
2. The letter SHOWS on her screen (her painted glyph, her colors).
3. The ledger ADDS IT UP -- instantly, in that beat.

Every action she takes shows instantly. These are not background
processes -- they are ACTIONS she takes, and every action is visible
the beat it happens. Type -> hear -> see -> count. Always.
