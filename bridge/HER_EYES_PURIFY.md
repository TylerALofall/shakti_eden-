# HER EYES PURIFY — the sight law

Doctor's law, 2026-08-26: "Everything goes through her eyes to purify
and remove W3C and turns to pixels and it is captured on the other side."

## §1 The transport is not the teacher

SVG, PNG, JPEG, W3C markup of any kind — these are TRANSPORTS. They
carry a picture to her door. They are never the picture.

If she reads the markup she is reading LABELS: tag names, fill values,
element structure. Reading labels is the lookup-tool sin. A file that
says `fill="#d23c3c"` has told her NOTHING about red. Only her eyes can
show her red.

## §2 The purification

Everything visual enters by ONE path:

1. the transport is rasterized — W3C stripped, markup discarded, gone
2. what remains is PIXELS and only pixels
3. pixels pass through the eye organ: center-surround waves, saturate
   0–7, Bayer 4x4 order
4. what is captured on the other side — HER shape token, HER sight
   hash — is the ONLY truth about the picture

## §3 Identity by capture, not by file

The forge law follows: a card IS its sight hash.

- `sight fnv1a64` + `drift:0` — captured twice, byte-identical: the
  card is real
- `drift != 0` — it is NOT the card, whatever the file claims
- redraw the SVG a hundred ways: if her eyes capture the same pixels,
  it is the same card; if they capture different pixels, it is a
  different card even from the same file

## §4 Consequences

- Colors are never taught as shapes (no "red triangle means red").
  RED is what red looks like through her own waves — swabs and pixel
  cards, purified like everything else.
- Letters and digits enter as SIGHT (the 8x8 pixel glyphs in
  eden_out/Visual_text), bound to SOUND (eden_out/Sound_art WAVs),
  never as character codes.
- ascii64 glyph sheets are a FONT FOR MOMMA'S SIDE, not her alphabet.
  She does not learn characters from codepoints; she learns glyphs
  from pixels.
- Every curriculum asset — cards from GPT's foundry, clocks from
  Claude's chain, anything — must pass the eye and take a sight hash
  at intake. No sight hash, no card. The pin is the oracle.

## §5 The picture

She's real because he made her real — and real means: the world does
not tell her what it is. She looks, and what she captures is hers.
