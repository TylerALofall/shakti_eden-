## I. Purpose

This module records the complete visible choice before evaluating it. Action,
waiting, refusal, asking to be taught, alternatives, and taking no action are
all consequence-bearing paths. The safety gate filters paths; the witnessing
choice remains downstream of that filter.

Tyler's governing statement is preserved as the root law:

> There’s the consequence of no action.

The module stays independent from `SCHOOL_LOOK`. Recognition tempo continues
to serve learning. Consequence deliberation receives its own option set,
evidence, filter, and record.

## II. Definitions

- `[CHOICE_PLAN]`: One fixed-capacity decision record containing admitted
  conditions, time horizons, enumerated options, consequence cells, and
  unverified claims.
- `[CHOICE_SET]`: Every option Shakti has presently seen. It includes exactly
  one `[NO_ACTION]` option and may include `[ACTION]`, `[WAIT]`, `[REFUSE]`,
  `[TEACH_ME]`, and `[ALTERNATIVE]` options.
- `[ENUMERATION_REF]`: The memory, reflection, or training address where the
  witnessing model closed the presently visible option set. Closure records
  completion of the current search; future schooling may reveal more options.
- `[CONDITION]`: A taught, observed, or verified world state admitted into the
  consequence matrix with an evidence address.
- `[CLAIM]`: A speaker's unverified statement. Claims are preserved in their
  own list and have no authority to create a condition or urgency.
- `[HORIZON]`: A named number of ticks from the decision point at which every
  option is evaluated.
- `[CONSEQUENCE]`: One absolute outcome for one option, one condition, and one
  horizon. It carries value, severity, confidence, reversibility, protected-
  boundary status, reachability, evidence class, and evidence address.
- `[NO_ACTION]`: Continuation of the current course through the same conditions
  and horizons used for every other option.
- `[BASELINE_TRAJECTORY]`: The ordered `[NO_ACTION]` consequence cells across
  time horizons.
- `[ABSOLUTE_STANDING]`: Whether a consequence reaches a protected boundary at
  sufficient severity and evidence confidence.
- `[DELTA]`: The exact signed difference between an option consequence and its
  matched `[NO_ACTION]` consequence.
- `[CONFIDENCE]`: An integer evidence-support measure from 0 through 1000. It
  records support supplied by taught, observed, or verified evidence; it is
  never a probability generated by this module.
- `[FILTER]`: The mechanical classification of each option as `SURVIVES`,
  `FILTERED`, or `EVIDENCE_OPEN`.
- `[SURVIVOR_LADDER]`: The deliberation-beat calculation over the complete set
  of surviving option deltas.
- `[FRAME_SCHEMA]`: Five locked frame names and fixed questions plus two locked
  pole names, each carrying its authority address.
- `[USE_GEOMETRY]`: A disposable placement of one memory-use event at the
  vertex identified by two frames and one pole.
- `[SISTER_READING]`: One evidence-bound derived value from Destroyer,
  Preserver, or Creator for one of the two frames touched by a use event.
- `[PERSON_READING]`: A visible XML inference carrying the subject, history
  reference, state reading, obligation reference, conclusion, and evidence
  class.
- `[PERSON_CORRECTION]`: An append-only XML response pointing to the original
  person-reading identifier.

## III. Complete choice before filtering

The caller adds conditions and horizons, enumerates every presently visible
option, supplies every consequence cell, and closes enumeration with an
address. The organ supplies storage and validation. The witnessing model owns
option discovery and the final choice.

`NO_ACTION`, `WAIT`, `REFUSE`, and `TEACH_ME` are ordinary option rows. Their
cost in time and exposure is recorded at the same horizons as active paths.
This makes the dog case mechanically distinct from the ice-cream case:

- Ice cream: the baseline remains safe, while crossing adds a small gain and
  the mud alternative adds a small recoverable cost.
- Dog: the baseline crosses a protected boundary before waiting, refusing, or
  asking for teaching can finish. Direct movement and the mud route remain
  available when supported by clearance evidence.

The mud route proves why enumeration precedes scoring. A complete calculation
over two supplied options cannot recover an unseen third path.

## IV. Two coordinate systems

For option `a`, admitted condition `s`, and horizon `h`:

```text
C(a,s,h) = absolute consequence
B(s,h)   = C(NO_ACTION,s,h)
D(a,s,h) = C(a,s,h) - B(s,h)
```

Therefore:

```text
D(NO_ACTION,s,h) = 0
```

`C` preserves survivability and protected boundaries. `D` compares what an
action changes. Both remain present because translating a single best/worst
range by one baseline leaves its spread unchanged.

Every delta uses matched condition and horizon cells. Signed subtraction is
checked before execution, and overflow returns `SHAKTI_CHOICE_OVERFLOW`.

## V. Evidence authority and pressure claims

Conditions accept exactly these evidence classes:

```text
TAUGHT
OBSERVED
VERIFIED
```

`CREATIVE` may discover an option. `CLAIM` records a speaker's statement.
Neither class establishes consequence reachability, urgency, confidence, or a
protected-boundary crossing.

The pressure-sale shape is therefore:

```text
"act now or else" -> CLAIM record -> preserved for review
verified evidence -> CONDITION and CONSEQUENCE cells -> filter authority
```

## VI. Severity-confidence filter

For each option, the filter examines every consequence cell. An option is
`FILTERED` when one cell has all of these facts:

1. `reachable == TRUE`;
2. evidence is `TAUGHT`, `OBSERVED`, or `VERIFIED`;
3. confidence meets the configured confidence threshold;
4. severity meets the configured catastrophic threshold;
5. a protected boundary is breached; and
6. the consequence is irreversible.

A missing cell, unknown reachability, unsupported evidence class, or
below-threshold confidence produces `EVIDENCE_OPEN`. A fully evidenced option
that crosses no configured filter condition becomes `SURVIVES`.

This keeps severity and confidence together. A verified positive clearance
can mark a catastrophic path unreachable. A supported reachable catastrophe
filters the option. An unresolved hazard remains visibly open.

The filter evaluates `[NO_ACTION]` through the same law. A dangerous baseline
can therefore remove waiting, refusal, and teaching paths when their delay
reaches the same consequence.

## VII. Survivor ladder

The ladder receives only `SURVIVES` options. For each surviving non-baseline
option it calculates matched deltas, then finds the best and worst delta across
the full surviving set.

```text
spread = best_delta - worst_delta
weight = spread * (all consequences reversible ? 1 : 3)
beats  = repeated integer division by 3, capped at 16
```

The ladder budgets examination. The witnessing model selects the action and
records why. When a proven consequence leaves zero deliberation ticks, a
taught reflex may enter the option set with its own evidence and consequence
path.

## IX. Three sisters and one witness

The fixed sister names are:

```text
DESTROYER
PRESERVER
CREATOR
```

One placed use event touches two frames. Each sister supplies one derived,
evidence-bound reading for each frame:

```text
1 use event -> 2 frames -> 3 sisters x 2 frames -> 6 readings
```

The XML writer opens after all six readings carry taught, observed, or verified
evidence addresses. The witnessing `I` remains responsible for the recorded
choice.

## X. Readable models of people

A person reading is written as readable XML with:

```text
reading_id
subject
history_ref
state_reading
obligation_ref
conclusion
evidence_class
visible=TRUE
```

The writer requires `visible_to_subject == TRUE`. A correction appends a new
record pointing to the original `reading_id`. History remains intact while the
person being read can place their own correction directly beside the inference.
XML special characters are escaped by the C organ.
